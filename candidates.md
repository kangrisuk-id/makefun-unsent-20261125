# Unsent: The Micro-Confession Sprint — candidates, scoring, tournament

Bounty: `u-boothlord-unsent-the-micro-confession-sprint-8c6596ba` — $2, 2 paid places,
deadline 26 Nov 2026 08:30 WIB. Entry = exactly two parts: a `To:` line (role, first
name or relationship; no real full names or handles) + the unsent message itself,
one line, exactly as typed, no explanation after it. Gate: UTF-8, 1–260 characters
total; judge brief caps the message at 200 characters.

## Judge brief — criteria in order (the spec)

1. **C1 — Two-part form.** A `To:` line + the message itself; anything else loses
   to any two-part confession.
2. **C2 — Felt reason it stayed in drafts.** Too honest, too late, too kind or too
   afraid; words specific and honest rather than general. The reason should be
   *felt in the words, not stated*.
3. **C3 — To: and message need each other.** Swapping the addressee would break it.
   A confession that would fit any addressee is not a confession.
4. **C4 — Reads as typed and never sent.** Pure jokes, greetings and motivational
   lines lose. Cleverness for its own sake does not count.
5. **C5 — One line, exactly as typed, no explanation after it.**

Scoring: each candidate 1–5 per criterion, in brief order. C1 is 5 for every
candidate below (all are two-part); the fight is C2 → C3 → C4 → C5.

## Devices used (8, ≥5 required)

- **A. Too late** — the moment passed, or they are gone.
- **B. Too honest** — a truth that would wound if sent.
- **C. Too kind** — protecting them by staying silent.
- **D. Too afraid** — saying it would break what exists.
- **E. Concrete object anchor** — one specific thing that only this addressee owns.
- **F. The substitute line** — what was actually said instead of the real sentence.
- **G. Confessed guilt** — a small, specific wrong, never admitted.
- **H. Gratitude never sent** — thanks that arrived too late to matter.

### Scoring notes (why the top scores differ)

- **#20 (21):** C4 = 3 — the plant swap reads as a punchline anecdote; the
  shock does the work, not the unsent feeling. Cleverness for its own sake.
- **#27 (20):** C2 = 3 — "the words I planned were better" is a mild regret, not
  a felt reason to stay silent; C3 = 3, it would fit almost any spouse.
- **#4 (21):** C3 = 3 — yearly birthday typing fits any lost friend; the addressee
  is interchangeable.
- **#24 (21):** C3 = 3 — "you fired me kindly" fits any decent first boss.
- **#16 (24) vs #13 (25):** the scarf is evocative but its closing line
  ("I can't wash it and I can't throw it away") is crafted and aphoristic —
  C4 = 4, it reads slightly written-to-be-read. #13's substitute line is plain
  speech, C4 = 5.
- **#26 (24):** C4 = 4 — "Dad still thinks it was me" is a clean confession but
  the setup explains itself a little too neatly; still a strong line.
- **#7 (24):** C4 = 4 — "I couldn't watch you marry him" is dramatic enough to
  feel slightly staged; the honesty is real, the delivery leans theatrical.
